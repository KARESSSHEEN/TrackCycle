ABOUT TRACKCYCLE

A clearer way to plan a local ride.

TrackCycle is a frontend tricycle seat reservation and queue management experience for BARANGAY A. Passengers can choose a pickup station and destination, review fares, and keep booking details close at hand.

Plan a ride
Designed for local journeys

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Simple tools for each part of the trip

Reserve a seat

Choose your station, destination, and passenger mix, then review the fare before confirming.

Follow the queue

See your saved queue status, passenger count, and departure countdown.

Keep trip records

Review booking and completed trip details from the History page.

HOW IT WORKS

From selection to boarding

Reservations are stored in this browser. TrackCycle uses pickup station to group active queues and shows their current local timer status.

    Choose your ride

    Select a pickup station and drop-off destination.

  2. 02

    Review the details

    Check passenger categories, discounts, and the fare summary.

  3. 03

    Follow the station queue

    Use Live Trip to check queue progress and the saved departure timer.
